公司介紹 Company Overview

歷史沿革 History

成立於 1980 年，為台灣第一家半導體公司。1995 年成功轉型為純晶圓代工廠 (Pure-play Foundry)，引領台灣半導體產業的專業分工模式。多年來深耕特殊製程，成為全球關鍵的晶圓製造夥伴。 Founded in 1980 as Taiwan's first semiconductor company. In 1995, it successfully transitioned to a pure-play foundry model, pioneering Taiwan's professional division of labor in the semiconductor industry.

組織架構 Organization

採專業經理人制，董事會下設共同總經理。事業層級涵蓋各大晶圓廠區（Fab），功能層級則包含研發(R&D)、全球業務、品管及 ESG 永續發展委員會，確保營運的高效與韌性。 Led by professional managers with Co-Presidents under the Board of Directors. Business units include major Fab operations, supported by functional departments like R&D, Global Sales, Quality Control, and ESG committees.

財務表現 Financials

近年專注於提升投資回報率 (ROI) 與維持健康的自由現金流。透過折舊完畢的成熟製程機台維持高毛利基礎，並發放穩定股息，展現穩健的財務體質。 Focuses on enhancing ROI and maintaining healthy free cash flow. Achieves solid gross margins through fully depreciated mature nodes and provides stable dividends, demonstrating financial resilience.

市場分析 Market Analysis

外部環境與競爭 (五力分析) External Environment & Competition

晶圓代工產業具有極高的資本與技術壁壘。競爭對手涵蓋台積電 (先進製程寡占)、中芯與格羅方德 (成熟製程競爭)。此外，地緣政治 (中美貿易戰) 促使供應鏈重組，成為聯電面臨的挑戰與機遇。 The foundry industry has high capital and tech barriers. Competitors include TSMC (advanced nodes), SMIC, and GlobalFoundries (mature nodes). Geopolitics drives supply chain restructuring, presenting both risks and opportunities.

需求驅動力 Demand Drivers

5G、物聯網 (IoT)、車用電子 (Automotive) 對於 28nm/22nm 等成熟製程與特殊製程 (如 RF, BCD) 的需求持續增長。 Consistent growth in demand for 28nm/22nm and specialty nodes (e.g., RF, BCD) driven by 5G, IoT, and Automotive electronics.

成長策略 Growth Strategy

  • 特殊製程領先 (Specialty Tech Leadership) Specialty Tech Leadership 不盲目追逐先進製程的資本競賽，轉而深耕 22/28 奈米特殊製程組合，強化獲利能力。 Avoiding the capital-intensive advanced node race, focusing instead on 22/28nm specialty portfolios to boost profitability.
  • 全球產能多元化 (Global Expansion) Global Expansion 在台灣、新加坡 (Fab 12i)、中國、日本佈局晶圓廠，為客戶分散地緣政治風險 (China+1 策略)。 Operating fabs in Taiwan, Singapore, China, and Japan to mitigate geopolitical risks for clients (China+1 strategy).
  • 策略結盟 (Strategic Alliances) Strategic Alliances 與客戶簽訂長約 (LTA) 綁定產能，並與上游 IP 供應商、設備商緊密合作。 Signing Long-Term Agreements (LTA) with clients and cooperating closely with IP/equipment suppliers.
